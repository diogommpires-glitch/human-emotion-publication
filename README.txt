Human Emotion
=================

README.md is the canonical setup, usage and limitations guide.

Use this folder's .venv. After following README.md, double-click
"Start Human Emotion.cmd" or run:

  .venv\Scripts\python.exe server.py

The app runs locally in your browser. Its activity traces and emotion-like
readouts are exploratory model proxies, not validated human measurements.
